## Installation

- Create an account — [Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/en/account/create){:target="_blank"}
- Save the **Account Number** in Bitwarden.
- Top up your account. Payment via **Monero** is recommended.

## Recommended servers

We recommend using **Germany** servers for the following reasons:

- **Mullvad** owns its servers in Germany (not rented)
- Germany has one of the best legal frameworks for personal data protection
- Germany is one of the central internet hubs, where many popular websites and
  application servers are located. This means that after connecting to VPN, access to
  desired websites will cause minimal additional latency.
