Often, managing security settings in operating systems and applications is difficult. Sometimes these settings are 
hidden, hard to find, or ambiguous. Changing them manually may take a long time or cause errors.

**FOI Security Policy** is a ready-made set of settings for various operating
systems and applications. By using it, your security settings will automatically 
be set to recommended values.

These settings are based on two sources:

1. [DISA STIG](https://public.cyber.mil/stigs/){:target="_blank"} standards, which are designed to protect US Department of Defense systems.
2. Our own recommendations, tailored to everyday use and real-world challenges.

**FOI Security Policy** will help you strengthen the security of your systems easily and quickly.
