**FOI Security** will help you improve your digital security
using simple instructions.

The site's policy is simple:

# Security should be simple and accessible.

Offering a wide range of choices to an inexperienced user
only makes things harder. Why is one Password Manager better than another? Because one 
uses PBKDF2 and the other Argon2? Is DNS-over-TLS better, or DNS-over-HTTPS? 

For most people, these terms mean very little, and they neither have the time to learn them
nor, let's be honest, the interest to do a comparative analysis afterwards.

That's why the choices in our solutions are minimized. 
We make decisions for you, based on our experience,
industry standards evaluation, comfort/benefit and risk analysis. 
We only offer you a choice when that choice is truly yours to make.
