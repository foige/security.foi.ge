# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 16, 2024.*

## Introduction
We value your personal information and are committed to protecting your privacy. 
This policy explains how we collect and use information on our website.


## Information Collection
We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io/){:target="_blank"}, an open-source analytics tool, 
to collect minimal information about our website usage. 
This information is completely anonymous and is stored within the European Union.

Collected data may include:

- Page URL
- HTTP referrer
- Browser
- Operating system
- Device type
- City


We also use Cloudflare for hosting our website. Cloudflare may collect 
certain data, such as IP addresses and traffic information, 
to ensure content delivery and security.

Please review [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/privacy-and-data-protection/){:target="_blank"} for additional information.

## Cookies
We do not use cookies on our website.

## Data Usage and Sharing

It should be noted that we use Plausible as a third-party service for analytics. 
Accordingly, the collected anonymous data is shared with Plausible for this service 
to function. [Plausible has its own privacy policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy){:target="_blank"}, 
which describes data security and privacy.


## Data Security

We take appropriate measures to protect collected information from unauthorized access, 
disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Analytics data is stored on servers 
within the European Union, which ensures strict data protection standards.
