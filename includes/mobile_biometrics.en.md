/// admonition | **Use** Biometric Authentication
    type: success

:material-fingerprint:&nbsp; Disabling **Face ID** / **Touch ID** (iOS/macOS) or **fingerprint** (Android) not only significantly reduces convenience but also pushes you toward using a simpler password

:material-alert-circle:&nbsp; This also increases the risk of password exposure, since you will need to enter it more frequently in public spaces

:material-lock:&nbsp; Your device's security depends on the **strength of its password**. Especially when the device is powered off

:material-fingerprint-off:&nbsp; After powering on from a shut-down state, Biometric Authentication won't work anyway until you enter the password at least once

:material-shield-check:&nbsp; Therefore, it is better to use a strong password combined with Biometric Authentication, and in case of a threat, simply power off the device

///
